## Final Decision [D]

I chose feature branch C. This adds a green to blue gradient face color and a curved smile to the portrait. This best
satisfies the criterion that was previously established, that the portrait should be expressive and have personality.
The gradient is distinctive and colorful and the smile gives the face character.

Feature A explored freckles and a pointed mohawd but the freckled didn't look natural and the mowhawk shapes didn't
work as well as I intended. Feature B explored sun and moon earrings, but the color of the sun earring blended in
with the color of the face and was hard to see. These alternatives were interesting to explore but option C provided
the best overall portrait.

Branches A and B are kept for reference. Feature branch C has been selected and brought to main.
